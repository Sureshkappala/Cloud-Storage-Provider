Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our technology? Check our answers below.

What is zero-knowledge encryption?

Zero-knowledge encryption is a standard security design where the service provider has zero information about the files and keys stored. The data is encrypted before leaving your devices, meaning no unauthorized personnel or database administrators can ever read or look into your files.

How does real-time synchronization work?

Our native synchronization client monitors file libraries for changes. When modifications happen, only the small delta bits (the altered parts of the document) are compressed, encrypted, and uploaded. This block-level delta sync engine cuts bandwidth waste and uploads changes instantly.

Can I share files with non-Stackly users?

Yes. You can generate secure, encrypted public sharing link keys. You can also specify access limits such as download limits, custom expiration dates, and password protection triggers. Recipient parties can download files directly from their browsers without setting up an account.

What happens if I exceed my plan's storage limit?

If you approach your storage threshold, you will receive an automatic email warning. If you exceed the limits, you can continue accessing and downloading files, but sync uploads will pause until you upgrade to a higher tier or free up space in your folders.
